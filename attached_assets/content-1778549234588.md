## How It Works

From photo to personalized preview in about 2 minutes!

### Step 1:  Upload a Photo

Provide just one clear photo of your child's face. Our AI studies their features, hair color, eye color, and skin tone, to make _them_ the star of their own book. You can even add a friend or siblings to really personalize it.

### Step 2:  Pick An Adventure

Choose from magical worlds, a mission across the stars, an adventure with friendly dinosaurs, an underwater journey, a royal quest, a brave rescue mission, or a whimsical enchanted quest. Then add your child's name and a few fun details.



### Step 3:  Create Their Book

Once your order is placed, Starmee AI writes a unique 12-page story and creates beautiful illustrations with your child as the star. Your eBook is then emailed to you and is instantly available to download, print, or share with family!

### You ASked,We Answered!

Is my child's photo safe?

We use the photo only to create the illustrations for your book. Your child’s photo stays private and is never shared with third parties. You can delete your child’s profile and photo at any time from your dashboard, and it will be permanently removed from our servers.

How long does it take?

You’ll see a preview in about 2 minutes. Final timing may vary slightly depending on image upload and generation speed. Once you purchase, the full high-resolution book with all illustrations is generated shortly after.

What ages is this for?

We have themes designed for children ages 2–12, including adventure stories, fairy tales, superhero origins, and heartfelt stories about kindness and courage.

Can I gift this?

We have themes designed for children ages 2–12, including adventure stories, fairy tales, superhero origins, and heartfelt stories about kindness and courage.

Will the illustrations look like my child?

The illustrations are stylized watercolor art inspired by your child’s photo features — hair color, skin tone, facial structure, and more. They won’t be photorealistic, but parents consistently recognize their child in the art. The style is warm, whimsical, and designed to feel like a hand-illustrated children’s book.

How do I access my book after purchasing?

After purchase, your book is available as an instant PDF download. It’s also permanently saved to your StorySpark account, so you can re-download it anytime from your dashboard. We’ll also email you a direct download link.

Can I print it at home?

Yes! The PDF is formatted for standard printing. You can print it at home on any color printer, or take it to a local print shop for professional-quality results.
